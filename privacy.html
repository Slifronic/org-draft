The Org System

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

The Org System is a tool a student club uses to sort its members into teams and track participation points. It is run by the club officers listed below, not by a company. This page explains, plainly, what it does with your information.

What it collects

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, and profile picture. Nothing else. The app never sees your password, and it cannot read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or contacts.
  • From your club's sign-up form: your name, how long you have been in the club, which track you lean toward, your MBTI type, whether you want to be an Org Lead, and any note you chose to write.
  • As the semester runs: which club events you attended and the points those earned.

Why it uses your Google account

Only to know who you are. Your email decides whether you see the page as a member, an officer, or the administrator. It is not used for marketing, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside your club.

Where it is kept

In a Google Sheet owned by the club officer who runs the system, and in your own browser's local storage so the page still works if you go offline. There is no separate company database and no third-party analytics.

Who can see what

  • Members see the standings, their own team, and their own points. They do not see the free-text notes anyone wrote on the sign-up form.
  • Officers see the full roster, including MBTI types and notes, because they need it to run the draft.
  • Nobody outside the club is given access.

About the MBTI field

MBTI is a self-reported questionnaire result, not a measurement of ability. The system uses it for one narrow purpose: spreading different types across teams so no team ends up all alike. It is never used to judge, rank, or exclude anyone. If you would rather not give a real result, give the type you would guess.

Your choices

You can ask the club officers to correct or delete anything the system holds about you, and they will do it. You can also sign out at any time, and revoke this app's access to your Google account entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
