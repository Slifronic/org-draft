# Org System — Fall 2026 setup

The Org System replaces the old Hub. It runs at https://org.deanzapbl.com (also https://org-draft.vercel.app)
(and on the chapter address). A walkthrough video is in
`media/setup-walkthrough.mp4`, and a safe sandbox is at `/demo`:

| Link | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| `/demo` | EBOD's view of a club mid-season |
| `/demo?as=member` | the same club as one member sees it |
| `/demo?as=new` | a brand-new member signing up and onboarding |
| add `&fresh=1` | start before any Orgs or teams exist |

Nothing in the demo is saved or sent anywhere. Use "Reset demo" in the demo bar to start over.

---

## What changed this season (EBOD decisions)

- **No titles.** No Associate / Analyst / Org Lead. Everyone is a **Member**; the only
  label next to a name is **1st year** or **2nd year**. 2nd years mentor inside their Org
  without a title.
- **Orgs** of 8–10, a mix of 1st and 2nd years, tracks and MBTI spread. Each Org has one
  **EBOD point of contact** (supervisory, low lift). Orgs are also the bootcamp breakout groups.
- **Teams.** Consulting: one team per client, 2 PMs, ~10–12 consultants split into
  initiatives. FLC: up to 4 per team, up to 3 teams per event. A member can be on one of each.
- **Points** are now a private *commitment log* on each member's profile, for reflection.
  Nothing ranks members. **Org Cups** replace the leaderboard: an Org earns a cup each time
  80% of its members hand an assignment in on time, or when EBOD awards one.
- **Season dates** are built into the Calendar: IM orientation Oct 15, bootcamp 1 + team
  formation Oct 20, bootcamp 2 (FLC / consulting split rooms) Oct 22, mock case stations the
  week of Oct 27, FLC Nov 13–15 (Emeryville), consulting finals Nov 19.

## For members

1. **Sign up.** Open the site → *Create an account* → *Sign up with Google* using your own
   email. Pick **De Anza PBL** and enter the join code EBOD gives you. Signed in with the
   wrong Google account? *Use a different account* on the join screen signs you out.
2. **Onboard (required).** Until you send the *Sign-up form*, it is the only page you can
   open: the other tabs are shown but locked, and a link to any of them opens the form
   instead (you land where the link pointed once it is sent). Answer year, track
   (Consulting / Case competition (FLC) / Polaris / Not sure yet), MBTI (there is a link to
   the test) and when you are usually available. Your answers are saved to your profile,
   and another device lets you straight in. Officers are never held, and a club with no
   form linked holds nobody. If the form will not load in the app, fill it in on Google
   and press *I sent it there — check again*.
3. **Take the walkthrough** that opens the first time you sign in (Orgs → Teams → Homework →
   Calendar → Org Cup → Profile).
4. **Competing at FLC?** On the **Teams** tab, rank your three events, optionally pick a
   teammate, and confirm you can be there Nov 13–15. Everyone going stays at the conference
   hotel, so there is no "need a bed" question: under **Hotel room**, pick a rooming group
   (women's / men's / all-gender), pick up to 3 roommates and add a note only EBOD sees.
   Going without competing? Leave the events empty. Press *Send my choices*. Your room
   appears there once EBOD sets it. Teammates and roommates are picked from a list of
   everyone who registered: start typing a name. Someone who has not registered can still be
   added with Enter; EBOD sees them flagged.
   **Already have a team?** *Form your own team*: pick FLC event, consulting client or
   track, name it, pick teammates from the list, *Send to EBOD for review*. It shows
   *Waiting*, *Approved* or *Sent back* (with EBOD's reason).
5. **After bootcamp 1 (Oct 20):** *Orgs* shows your Org, its 2nd years and your EBOD contact.
   *Teams* shows your consulting team (PMs, your initiative) and FLC team.
6. **Homework:** open *Homework*, upload a file or paste a shared link before the due date.
   Re-handing in replaces your earlier version; the first on-time hand-in still counts.
   **Phone notifications:** open the Org System from its home-screen icon, then *Homework →
   Phone notifications → Turn on notifications* and allow them. You hear about new homework set
   for you, get a reminder at 6 pm the day before something is due if you have not handed it in,
   and get a receipt when your hand-in arrives. On iPhone this only works from the home-screen
   icon (iOS 16.4 or newer); on Android it also works in Chrome.
7. **Calendar:** shows everything on the chapter's Google Calendar that EBOD has not hidden,
   kept up to date on its own. Every event has *+ Calendar* to add it to your own Google
   Calendar, and *Add the org calendar* (once EBOD has shared it) subscribes you to it.
8. **Not sure how a tab works?** Press *Watch* at the top of it for a short captioned clip.
   All of them are also on `/orientation` under *Tab by tab*.

## For EBOD

All EBOD sign in with the **PBL Google account** (or their own address once granted
*admin* on the Access tab by the master account).

**Before orientation (once)**
1. *Intake → Club setup*: tracks should read Consulting, Case competition (FLC), Polaris.
   If the live club still shows the old names, edit them there (or "Load kit" →
   Business / PBL, which also resets the points catalog).
2. *Intake → The sign-up form's questions*: make sure the form asks year (1st/2nd), track,
   MBTI and availability. Members fill it in inside the app; answers land in the linked sheet
   and on their profile.
3. Share https://org.deanzapbl.com and the join code.

**Bootcamp 1 — form Orgs (Oct 20)**
1. *Intake → Roster source → Signed-up members → Use signed-up members.* The mapping fills
   itself; the Org count is suggested so each Org has 8–10.
2. *Run draft.* Check the *Draft* tab: each card shows the track bar, the 2nd/1st split and
   the type spread. Red border = needs a manual look.
3. *Assign EBOD contacts* (round-robin), then change any from the card's dropdown.
4. Move people on each Org's page with the *Move* dropdown. *Reshuffle draft* starts over.

**Choosing your tabs.** Officers see a lot of tabs. *Edit tabs* (right end of the tab bar)
lists them with checkboxes: untick the ones you rarely use and they move under *More*, one
click away. It is saved for your account in that browser. Members never see officer-only
tabs at all.

**Teams**
1. *Teams → Set up every client team and place consulting-track members.* Then on each card
   pick the two PMs, type the initiatives (comma-separated) and set each consultant's initiative.
   Every spot on a card is a dropdown of everyone registered: pick a name in someone's spot to
   swap them, *Take off this team* to empty it, or fill an empty spot (an FLC team always shows
   all 4 spots; other teams show one spare). Picking someone already on another team of the
   same kind moves them, and the message says from where. The note above the cards lists who
   is not on any team yet.
2. The *FLC preferences* panel lists everyone who sent choices. *Form FLC teams from
   preferences* fills each event's teams from first, then second, then third choices, keeps
   requested teammates together where it can, and lists anyone it could not place. Selection
   criteria are still EBOD's call — adjust by hand (an FLC card stops at 4).
3. *Build a team* adds a single consulting / FLC / other team.
4. *Teams members sent in* lists self-formed teams. *Change members* on any of them, waiting
   or approved, turns its members into dropdowns; on an approved one, saving also changes the
   team it became (and moves anyone added off their other team of that kind). A request now
   shows the sender's registered name rather than their email. Each shows what to check (not on the
   roster, already on a team of that kind, over the size limit, event already has 3 teams,
   said they can't go, in another waiting request). *Approve* makes the team (consulting
   members join that client's team; anyone on another team of that kind is moved) or
   *Send back…* with a reason. Untick the box at the top to stop members sending teams.
5. *FLC hotel rooms*: everyone going gets a bed. Set people per room, *Form rooms from requests* (one rooming group
   per room, requested roommates together, as few rooms as possible), then move people,
   change a room's group and type the hotel room number at check-in. *Download FLC teams
   and rooms (CSV)* gives the registration / hotel list. Rooming notes are officers-only.

**Homework**
1. *Homework → Set an assignment*: start from a template or blank, set a due date, choose
   who it is for (everyone, one Org, one team) and whether it is a file, a link, or either.
   A few seconds later it appears as a subfolder in the Drive folder of everyone it is for.
   **Member Drive folders**: every member has a folder ("<club> — member folders" in the
   PBL account's Drive) with a subfolder per assignment, and their hand-ins are filed there.
   The folders are for officers and are not shared with members. A folder is made when a
   member first opens the app; *Set up folders for everyone* does the whole club at once. The
   usual "<club> — handed in / assignment / Org / name" folders still get a shortcut to every file.
2. Under each assignment: handed in / on time / late / still to come, the names not in yet,
   and each hand-in with *Check off* (optional commitment points).

**Attendance** (its own tab): *Take attendance* checks people in for one event. *Who was
absent* lists everyone on the roster who was not checked in: pick *One meeting*, or *A date
range* to see who missed the most (choose which meetings count and a minimum, e.g. missed at
least 2). *Copy names* and *Download CSV* take the list into an email or the sheet.

**Org Cups** are automatic from on-time homework; *Award a cup* on the Org Cup tab for
anything else (turnout, a mock case win).

**Calendar**: the page shows the whole connected Google Calendar, so members see every
event on it **unless you hide it**. The field beside *Connect & refresh* holds the chapter
calendar; on the PBL club it suggests **deanzacapbl@gmail.com**. Press *Connect & refresh*
once to link it. After that it refreshes itself every half hour (30 days back, about 8
months ahead), so nothing needs pressing for members to see new events.
- **Hide** on any event keeps that one occurrence from members; **Hide every one** on a
  repeating event (EBOD meetings, ICC) hides all of it, future ones included. *Show to
  members* / *Show every one* undoes it. Hidden events stay visible to officers, marked.
- **Preview as a member** shows the calendar exactly as members see it.
- The line under the calendar says what is connected, how many events are shared and
  hidden, and when it last refreshed.
- Adding an event here puts just that event on the Google Calendar straight away.
  Built-in season dates go across only when you press *Connect & refresh* (an event already
  there under much the same name, e.g. "IM Orientation MEETING", is matched, not duplicated).
- Meeting links come back as "Online". Multi-day events (breaks, conferences) show on every
  day. The linked calendar's sharing is never changed.
- Upgrading from the earlier version: events that used to come back as "EBOD only" are now
  shared. Open the calendar once and hide what members should not see.

**Shareable org calendar link**: under *Shareable link to the org calendar*, paste the
calendar's share link (Google Calendar → the calendar's *Settings* → *Integrate calendar*:
the public URL, or the iCal address for an `.ics` feed) and *Save link*. *Use the synced
calendar* fills in the synced one. Members then get *Add the org calendar* and *Copy link*.
The link only works for people who can see that calendar, so make it public or share it
with the chapter under *Access permissions*. If it is the synced calendar and it holds
EBOD-only events, the page warns you that anyone the link works for sees those too —
consider a separate members' calendar for sharing.

**Tab demo clips** live in `media/demos/`; `tools/demos/` re-records them.

## The dashboard (`/dash`), Oct 28, 2026

A second front end beside the classic page, for working on a dashboard-style UI without
touching `index.html`. Same sign-in (it reads the session the classic page keeps in this
browser), same backend, same club record. Files: `dash.html` and `dash-app/`
(`core.js` data and tags, `views.js` screens, `app.js` clicks, `demo.js` the demo club,
`app.css`). Desktop gets a sidebar; under 860px it switches to a bottom tab bar and
bottom sheets.

| Link | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| `/dash` | the live club (sign in on `/` first) |
| `/dash?demo` | an officer's view of a made-up club two weeks before SFBLC |
| `/dash?demo&as=member` | the same club as a member (Alex, on Finance 1) |

Locally the Canopy preview serves `dist/`, so run `node build.mjs` and open `/dash.html?demo`.

**What is in it**
- **Overview**: days to SFBLC, counts, and *Close these before SFBLC*: every gap it can
  see (event list vs SFBLC 2026, the Oct 30 registration deadline, members going with no
  team, over-full teams, events with no coaching, no hotel rooms, people without phone
  notifications, missing sign-up answers, the club record nearing the 50,000-character cell
  limit). Each has a button that fixes it or opens the place to.
- **Members**: tags worked out from the sheet: year, track, Org, consulting client,
  SFBLC event, rooming group, availability, type, wants to lead, plus officer-made tags
  (`memberTags`, never sent to members) and *Needs attention* tags (no sign-up answers,
  missing or late homework, missed 2+ meetings, no phone notifications, going with no team,
  no hotel room). Filter by any mix, select people, tag them, message them, export CSV.
- **SFBLC**: schedule (`sfSchedule`), coaching sessions (`sfCoaching`), checklist
  (`sfChecklist`, members tick items off; officers see who has not and can remind them),
  and About (venue, dates, fees, events, team sizes in `sfInfo.caps`). *Load SFBLC 2026
  starter* adds the three known days without times, the Oct 30 registration item and a
  short checklist. Times stay empty until CAPBL's programs packet is out.
- **Teams**: the SFBLC teams on the club record (the same `teams` the classic Teams tab
  edits), by event, with move / add / remove, *Place by first choice* and team size limits.
- **Messages**: compose to any tags or names, preview who it reaches and who has
  notifications on, send now or schedule, optionally email too. *Automations* turn on the
  day-before and hour-before reminders, checklist-deadline nudges and team placement
  notices. Members get an **Inbox**.

**Every classic tab, on the dashboard (Oct 29).** Orgs (cards, EBOD contact, on-time
rate), Homework (members hand in a file or link; officers set assignments, see in / on time /
late / missing and remind the missing), Calendar (month and agenda: chapter Google Calendar,
events added here, season dates, SFBLC schedule, coaching, homework due dates), Org Cup
(standings and *Award a cup*), Attendance (take it, see who was absent, message them),
Profile (name, phone notifications, commitment log, look) and Officer tools (deep links to
the classic Intake, Roster, Draft, Sign-up form, Access, Assistant, rooms and team requests).
On phones the bottom bar is Home, SFBLC, Homework, Inbox and *More*.

**Look.** The dashboard wears deanzapbl.com by default (*Φ PBL*: `#0a0a0a`, `#f5f3ef`,
`#c8102e`, IBM Plex Mono labels, Playfair's Φ). *PBL
light* is the same on cream; *Org dark* and *Org light* are the classic page's. The choice
is kept per browser under `orgsystem.dash.theme`, apart from the classic page's.

**Home screens.** Officers: a greeting with New message / Set homework / Take attendance,
four figures (days to SFBLC, placed on a team, gaps to close, homework on time for the last
assignment), then *Needs attention*, *This week* (the next seven days from every calendar
source, in time order), Homework progress, Org Cup and the SFBLC checklist. Members: days to
SFBLC with their team, homework (overdue flagged), checklist and inbox, then *Your week*,
their SFBLC team and room, open homework, checklist, their Org (cups, place, EBOD contact)
and messages. Progress bars are neutral, green at the mark (80% earns a cup), amber when an
assignment fell short; red is kept for things to fix.

**Uniform cards.** Cards that sit side by side share one width (the grid) and one height,
set in `dash-app/app.css` (`--h` 380px for home and schedule cards, `--h-sm` 300px for
homework and the unplaced list, `--h-lg` 520px for team columns and the calendar; a little
shorter on phones). Longer content scrolls inside the card with its title pinned and a fade
at the edge; on phones a card that reaches its end hands the swipe back to the page. One
figure style (DM Sans bold) is used for every number, avatars are neutral, and actions inside
cards are quiet text buttons (`.qbtn`).

**Readability (Oct 29).** Every text colour passes WCAG AA on every surface it sits on, in all
four looks (muted text 5.1:1 or more, small red text 6.1:1 or more; the fill red `#c8102e` is
only used behind white text, as a border, or for large figures). Black and white are softened
(`#0e0e0f` / `#ebe8e2`) to cut glare. All-caps mono is limited to short labels at 12px or more;
buttons, tags and navigation are sentence case. *Profile → Look and comfort* adds **Text
size** (Standard / Large / Larger) and **Motion** (Full / Calm; Calm is the default when the
device asks for reduced motion and stops the scrolling band and fades). **Search** (top bar,
⌘K / Ctrl+K or `/`) finds people, teams, tags, schedule items, homework, screens and actions.

**Linked sheets and Discord (Officer tools → Linked sheets & Discord).** The dashboard reads
the chapter's other sheets through the new `linkedRead` action (officers only, only the

**Built around how PBL runs (Oct 29).** The navigation follows the chapter: *Chapter*
(Overview, Calendar, Messages, EBOD), *Org Program* (Orgs, Homework, Org Cup),
*Competitions* (SFBLC with a *Season* tab for SFBLC → SBLC → NLC and last season's results,

- **EBOD**: the Executive Board and Directors (from `ebod` on the club record, else the

## One-time backend update (Bryant)

`index.html` deploys with Vercel as before. The new features that need the sheet —
FLC preferences, availability, link hand-ins, Org Cup numbers, onboarding answers on the
club roster — need the updated **`apps-script/Code.gs`**:

1. Open the "Org System Data" sheet → Extensions → Apps Script.
2. Replace the code with `apps-script/Code.gs` from this branch and save.
3. Deploy → Manage deployments → edit the web app → *New version* → Deploy (keep the same
   URL so `WEBAPP_URL` in `index.html` does not change).

**Drive folders update (Oct 2026).** After deploying, run `checkDriveIntegrations` from the
editor to test every Drive path, and `setUpMemberFolders` to give every existing member a folder.

**Phone notifications for members (Oct 22, 2026) — five steps, in this order.** Members get
a push notification when homework is posted for them (everyone, their Org, their team, their
track), at 6 pm the day before it is due if they have not handed it in, and when their own
hand-in arrives. New posts and reminders go out from the 10-minute `keepWarm` trigger; the
receipt goes out as the hand-in is saved. The first run only takes note of what is already
posted, so nothing old is announced. Apps Script cannot encrypt push messages, so the site's
own `api/push` function (Vercel) sends them; the backend calls it with a shared secret.

1. **Keys.** In this folder run `npx web-push generate-vapid-keys` (a public and a private key)
   and `openssl rand -base64 32` (the shared secret). Keep the private key and secret out of
   git and out of chat.
2. **Vercel** → the org-draft project → Settings → Environment Variables (Production):
   `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `VAPID_SUBJECT` = `mailto:deanzacapbl@gmail.com`,
   `PUSH_SECRET`. Then deploy. `https://org.deanzapbl.com/api/push` should answer with
   `"publicKey"`; before the variables are set it answers "Push is not set up".
3. **Google Cloud** → APIs & Services → Credentials → the OAuth client in `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`
   → *Authorized redirect URIs*: add `https://org.deanzapbl.com/api/signin` and
   `https://org-draft.vercel.app/api/signin`. The home-screen icon now opens the site as its own
   app (the manifests say `standalone`), where Google's sign-in popup cannot answer back, so
   there sign-in goes through Google's full-page redirect to that address. Without this step,
   signing in from the icon shows Google's redirect_uri_mismatch error.
4. **Apps Script** (as deanzacapbl): paste `Code.gs`, deploy a new version, then Project
   Settings → Script properties → add `PUSH_SECRET` with the same value as in Vercel. No new
   permission is asked for. Until it is set, nothing is sent and hand-ins are not slowed.
5. **Members who already added the icon** must delete it and add it again: an iPhone keeps
   opening an existing icon in Safari, and only the app window can get notifications. Then
   *Homework → Phone notifications → Turn on*; a test notification arrives at once, and
   *Send a test* repeats it.

The home-screen app signs a member in once and keeps a 30-day session (a `g1.` token signed
with the same secret as password sessions), renewed every time the app opens, so members are
not sent back to Google every hour. Devices live on the new **Push** tab of the data sheet,
one row per device; a device the push service reports gone is removed on the next send, and
signing out turns notifications off on that device.

**Homework alerts (Oct 22, 2026) — redeploy, then approve mail once.** Officers get an email
listing new hand-ins (who, Org, assignment, on time or late, replaced versions, a link to the
file), at most one every 10 minutes, sent by the `keepWarm` trigger as deanzacapbl. The first
run only notes the time, so nothing already handed in is sent. Set it on the **Homework** tab →
*Homework alerts*: on by default, and with no addresses it goes to the club Google account.
After pasting the new `Code.gs` and deploying, run **`authorizeScopes`** as deanzacapbl and
approve the new *Send email as you* permission (it should print `Mail: OK`). Until then the
trigger logs a permission error and sends nothing; the warm-up still runs. To see an alert,
run **`handinAlertTest`**: it emails each club's latest three hand-ins, marked [Test].
A personal Gmail account can email 100 recipients a day, which batching keeps well clear of.
