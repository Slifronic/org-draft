Connecting your Google Sheet

This tool runs entirely in your browser — there's no server. So to read your sheet directly, the sheet has to be readable without a login. Two ways to do that:

Option A — Publish just the responses (recommended)

  1. Open your Form's response sheet. Make a new tab containing only the columns the draft needs — name, year, track, lead interest, notes. Leave emails out of it.
  2. File → Share → Publish to web. Choose that tab, format Comma-separated values (.csv), then Publish.
  3. Copy the link it gives you and paste it into the Google Sheet tab above.
  4. Unpublish it after the draft — File → Share → Publish to web → Stop publishing.

Option B — Link sharing

Set the sheet to Anyone with the link → Viewer and paste the normal sheet URL. Same privacy tradeoff, less setup. The tool converts the URL automatically.

If the connection is blocked

Google occasionally refuses cross-site reads depending on how the sheet is shared. If that happens the tool will say so — use File → Download → CSV and drop the file into the Upload tab instead. Same result, thirty seconds more work, and nothing gets published.

The privacy call

Publishing a tab of ~100 classmates' names makes it readable by anyone with the link. Names and class years are low-risk; emails and phone numbers are not. Keep contact details off the published tab, and unpublish when you're done. If you'd rather publish nothing at all, use CSV upload — it's just as fast and the file never leaves your computer.

What to collect at sign-up
  1. Name — full name, one per member.
  2. Email — for the Lead to reach them. Not used by the sort.
  3. Year in the club — first year (new member) or second year and up. Ask about club tenure, not academic standing: a junior who just joined is a first-year for draft purposes.
  4. Track lean — consulting, case competitions, or either. Keep the third option: undecided members are the flex pieces that let the sort even out lopsided groups.
  5. Lead interest — yes/no, returning members only. The draft guarantees each Org gets co-Leads.
  6. Anything the draft should know — optional free text. Members with a note get flagged in the results so you can hand-adjust.

You don't need to reformat any of this. The tool reads raw Google Form exports and matches the columns itself.
