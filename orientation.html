What the system is for

A chapter with sixty members has a problem a chapter with fifteen does not: nobody can hold the whole roster in their head. Who is in which Org, who competes at SFBLC, who has handed in nothing since October. The Org System answers that without a spreadsheet nobody wants to maintain.

It keeps the chapter in one place: your Org, your homework, your SFBLC and client teams, the calendar, messages from EBOD, and the Org Cup your Org earns together. Officers run the same page with more tools.

Getting in

Open org.deanzapbl.com and sign in with the Google account you gave PBL, or with a password if you set one. What you can see depends on the role officers gave you, not on anything you can set yourself.

  1. Sign in. Google is the normal door. The page receives your name, email and profile picture, and never your Google password.
  2. Join De Anza PBL. The first time, pick the chapter and type the join code EBOD gives you at your first meeting.
  3. Send the sign-up form. It opens first, and the rest of the site unlocks once it is in. Officers place you in an Org from your answers, so answer every question.
  4. Put it on your home screen. It then opens like an app and keeps you signed in. On an iPhone, phone notifications only work from the home-screen icon.

Stuck in any section? Press Watch at the top of it for a short clip of how it works.

Members and officers

Roles are set by officers and checked by the backend every time anything is read or saved. Hiding a button is not what keeps things private: the backend only sends a member what a member may see.

Every section, in order

The side bar (on a phone, the bar at the bottom and More) runs in this order. Officers see more sections and more tabs inside them.

Overview Everyone

Your home page: notices from EBOD, a count of what needs you, your week, your SFBLC team, your homework and your checklist.

Calendar Everyone

Meetings, SFBLC, coaching and homework due dates, month by month. Tap a day to see what is on and where.

Links Everyone

The Discord, the attendance ticket, forms, folders and calendars, grouped by what they are for.

Inbox Everyone

Every message EBOD sends you, newest first. Turn on phone notifications on your Profile so they reach you.

EBOD and Org chart Everyone

Who is on the board, your Org's EBOD contact, and who to ask for an extension or an absence. The Org chart shows the whole chapter.

Orgs Everyone

Six Orgs of 8 to 10 members, a mix of 1st and 2nd years, each with one EBOD contact. Yours comes first.

Homework Everyone

Every assignment for your track, soonest due first. Hand in a file or a link; only EBOD sees it. Ask for an extension from here.

Org Cup Everyone

Cups each Org has earned, together. Nobody is ranked.

SFBLC Everyone

The conference, Nov 13–15: the schedule (with Mine for just yours), coaching, your checklist, carpools, and Need to know with the rules and the walking radius.

Competition teams Everyone

Your SFBLC team and every other one. Under Your choices, rank your events and say who you would like to room with.

Client teams Everyone

The consulting track: your client team, its two PMs, its stage and initiatives, and your client ranking.

Internal Members Everyone

Everyone in the chapter, searchable and filterable by tag: event, Org, track, who is going to SFBLC.

Profile Everyone

Your details, picture, phone notifications, commitment log and standing. See Your own page.

Messages, Attendance, Club Officers

Composing and scheduling messages and automatic reminders, taking attendance and deciding tickets, and setting the club up: sign-up form, roster, calendar and positions.

Officer tools Officers

The linked Google Sheets that sync both ways (attendance, SFBLC teams, rooms and itinerary, carpools), the Discord link-up and the club record.

How the Org Cup works

The Cup is a race between Orgs, not between people. There is no leaderboard of members.

  • On-time cups. An Org earns a cup each time 80% of its members hand an assignment in on time.
  • Awarded cups. EBOD can award a cup for something the rule does not capture, such as full turnout or winning the mock case stations.

Members see how many cups each Org has and how often it hands in on time, never who in an Org was late.

Homework, meetings and your standing

The Internal Member contract, as the Org System applies it. Your standing on your Profile shows where you are.

  • Can't make a meeting? Tell an officer at least 3 hours before, with an attendance ticket on Discord. More than 2 unexcused meetings in a row means removal from the membership class.
  • Late homework without an extension. The first is a warning, the second a meeting with the President or Vice President, the third a letter of resignation.
  • Extensions are asked for at least 3 days before the deadline, from the President or Chapter Adviser. You can ask from the assignment on Homework.
  • Code of conduct. A first offense is a formal warning; a second, being asked to resign.

Your own page

Profile is the page written for you rather than for an officer. It has:

  • Your details: track, year, personality type, birthday and anything EBOD should know. You can read them back from the sign-up form with one button.
  • Phone notifications, so EBOD's messages and reminders reach you.
  • Your commitment log: a private record of what you have put in this season, for you and EBOD. It is not a ranking.
  • Your standing under the member contract, and your positions and trophies.
  • What others can see, a password if you would rather not use Google, and the look of the page (theme, text size, motion).

Another member's type, points, notes and hand-ins are theirs. Officers see them because running the chapter requires it; other members do not.

What officers do in a semester

In order, so members know what is coming and officers know what is theirs.

Week 0

Set the club upClub setup, the sign-up form, the calendar and positions. Hand out the join code at the first meeting.

Weeks 1–2

Collect sign-upsMembers join and send the form, and the roster fills in on its own. Chase whoever has not: Orgs are drafted from the answers.

Week 2

Draft OrgsSix Orgs balanced on year and track, then the recommended swaps for personality types. Every Org gets an EBOD contact.

All term

Run the weekTake attendance at every meeting and decide tickets, post homework and review hand-ins, and send messages. Cups and standing update themselves.

Before SFBLC

Teams, rooms, scheduleForm teams and rooms from members' choices, fill in the schedule and coaching, set the checklist and carpools. Teams, rooms and the itinerary sync with the SFBLC master sheet both ways.

What is stored, and where

Everything lives in the chapter's own Google account: a Google Sheet for the club's records and Drive folders for hand-ins. There is no separate database and no third party holding the roster.

  • Your sign-up answers and details: year, track, type, availability.
  • Attendance, hand-ins and your commitment log, readable by you and officers only.
  • Messages EBOD sent you, and your carpool answers. Your phone number and pickup address are shared only with officers and the people in your car.
  • Your name and picture, if you set them.

Chapters are separate from one another: separate roster, teams and officers. Nothing crosses between them.
